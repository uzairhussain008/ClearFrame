How Your Videos and Photos Are Processed

ClearFrame processes media locally or temporarily on the server depending on the tool and mode you choose:

  • Photo Watermark Remover & Photo Upscaler (100% Browser Processing): Your photos (JPG, PNG, WebP up to 25 MB) are decoded, inpainted, or upscaled entirely inside your own browser using client-side JavaScript/WebGPU/WASM. The first time HD Mode is used, your browser downloads the AI model file from huggingface.co (your photos are never uploaded). Photos are never uploaded to ClearFrame servers and never leave your device.
  • Uploaded Video Processing (Default CPU Mode): When you upload a video (up to 300 MB), the file is transferred to the ClearFrame server's temporary workspace so the server can read metadata, apply watermark removal filters, and run CPU upscaling before delivering your final MP4.
  • Browser WebGPU AI Video Mode: When you select the AI Upscaler option on a supported browser, video frame super-resolution runs locally inside your browser using WebGPU/WASM (and the AI model weights are cached locally in your browser storage). The upscaled video stream is then uploaded to the server briefly only to combine it with your original audio track into an MP4 file.

Temporary Retention & Automatic Deletion

Photo files are never uploaded or retained on our servers. For uploaded videos, source files and processed MP4 outputs are kept only in temporary server storage while you edit, preview, and download. All temporary video files are automatically deleted after 15 minutes, or immediately when you switch or reset videos. ClearFrame does not sell, publish, or use your uploaded media to train AI models.

Analytics, Advertising & Cookies

ClearFrame requires no account or signup and collects no personal registration details. The website uses Google Tag Manager to measure basic traffic and third-party ad networks to keep the service free. Those third-party scripts may set standard browser cookies or collect anonymous usage metrics under their own privacy policies.
