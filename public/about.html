About ClearFrame

ClearFrame is a free web-based video cleanup and upscaling studio built for creators, editors, and everyday users who need clean exports without subscriptions, credit paywalls, or added export watermarks.

What ClearFrame Offers

Upload MP4, MOV, WebM, or MKV videos up to 300 MB to erase unwanted logos, captions, or timestamps across up to 8 independent regions, or enhance footage up to 1080p, 2K, and 4K UHD while keeping your original audio track in sync.
